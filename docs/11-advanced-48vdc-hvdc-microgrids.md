# Chapter 11 — 48VDC, HVDC & DC Microgrids

## Overview

The industry is shifting from 12V/208V AC distribution to 48VDC at the rack and HVDC at the facility level. This chapter covers the standards, topologies, and why DC is winning.

## Key Concepts (To Be Developed)

- Open Compute 48V rack standard (OCP ORv3)
- ±400V HVDC backbone vs 3-phase AC
- DC microgrid integration with renewables and battery ESS
- Conversion loss reduction: fewer AC/DC stages
- Protection in DC systems: DC breakers, fuses, arc extinction
