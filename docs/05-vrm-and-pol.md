# Chapter 5 — Voltage Regulator Modules (VRM) & Point-of-Load

> 🚧 **Status:** Architecture placeholder — content to be populated.

## Overview

The VRM is the last stage of power conversion before the GPU die. It must maintain voltage within ±50mV while the load current swings by hundreds of amps in microseconds.

## Key Concepts (To Be Developed)

- Multi-phase VRM architecture (N-phase interleaved buck)
- AVP (Adaptive Voltage Positioning) — deliberate load-line to minimize droop
- Transient response specs: overshoot, undershoot, settling time
- PMBus communication and telemetry
- Integrated vs discrete VRM solutions (MPS, Infineon, TI)
