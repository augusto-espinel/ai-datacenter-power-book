## Overview

The power density of AI training racks has increased by 10× in three years. This chapter establishes the landscape:

- **2018–2020:** CPU racks at 5–10 kW/rack, air-cooled, 208V single-phase
- **2021–2023:** GPU accelerators (A100) at 20–40 kW/rack, 3-phase 415V, liquid-assisted
- **2024–2026:** H100/B200 clusters at 80–130 kW/rack, direct liquid cooling, 800VDC bus
