## Core Electrical & Circuit Standards Referenced in This Companion

- **MV/LV Switchgear, Short-Circuit & Arc Flash:** IEC 60909 (Short-circuit currents), IEC 61439 (Low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies), IEEE C37.20, IEEE 1584-2018 (Arc-Flash Hazard Calculations), NFPA 70E.
- **Transformers & Harmonics:** IEC 60076 series, IEEE C57.110 (Recommended Practice for Establishing Transformer Capability When Supplying Nonsinusoidal Load Currents), OCP Solid-State Transformer (SST) Specification v0.3.
- **LV & DC Installations / Conductor Sizing:** IEC 60364-5-52 / HD 60364 (Selection and erection of electrical equipment — Wiring systems), Swiss NIN (SN 411000), NFPA 70 (NEC Table 310.16), OCP ORv3 & Mt. Diablo ($\pm 400\text{ VDC}$ / $800\text{ VDC}$).
- **UPS, Energy Storage & Safety:** IEC 62040-3 (UPS performance and test requirements), UL 1778, UL 9540 / UL 9540A (BESS safety and thermal runaway), IEC 62619.
- **IT Power Delivery & Telemetry:** IEC 62368-1, PMBus Power System Management Protocol Specification (Part I & II), VESA/Intel SVID, JEDEC JESD301.
- **Facility Efficiency Metrics:** ISO/IEC 30134-2 (PUE), ISO/IEC 30134-6 (ERF), ISO/IEC 30134-9 (WUE).
